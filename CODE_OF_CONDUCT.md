# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Give constructive, specific feedback, welcome different experiences and keep discussion focused on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or remove access when needed to protect the community.

Report community concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not publish private details in an issue. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and avoid retaliation.
